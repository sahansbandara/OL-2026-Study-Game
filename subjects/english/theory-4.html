💬 Reported speech — backshift

Direct: “I am tired,” said Ravi. → Reported: Ravi said (that) he was tired.

  • Pronouns change with the speaker: “I like it,” said Pasan → Pasan said he liked it.
  • Universal truths keep their tense: He said the sun rises in the east.
  • Commands: “Sit down,” said the teacher. → The teacher told us to sit down.
💡

Memory trick — “one step back”: move every verb one tense back, like stepping back on a staircase; change I/you/my to match who is speaking.

🧪 Self-check: Report: Rajini said, “I will visit you tomorrow.”

Rajini said (that) she would visit me the next day.

❓ Reported questions and statements

Wh-questions

Use statement word order (no do/does/did, no question mark).

“What is the name of the actress?” → Naveen wanted to know what the name of the main actress was. (2019-style)

Yes/No questions

Use if / whether.

“Did you like the film?” → She asked if he had liked the film.

Direct questionReported
“Where do you live?”He asked me where I lived.
“Are you ready?”She asked if/whether I was ready.
“When will the train come?”He wanted to know when the train would come.
⚠️

Common mistakes: ✗ He asked where did I live. ✓ He asked where I lived. ✗ She asked did I like it. ✓ She asked if I liked it. Never put a question mark after a reported question.

🧪 Self-check: Report: “Why are you late?” the teacher asked Nimal.

The teacher asked Nimal why he was late.

🎯 How the question comes: Test 10 (2019 format): six direct sentences by Ravi / Pasan / Naveen to complete in reported speech; or Test 12 (2023) with Jaleel/Rajini. Marks go for correct backshift + word order + spelling.
Test 10 (2020) Frequent in exams

🧐 Question formation — writing the question

2020 Test 10 gave a paragraph about Dr Arthur C. Clarke with the answer part underlined. You write the question.

Formula: Wh-word + auxiliary (do/does/did/is/was/has) + subject + main verb?

Statement (answer underlined)Question
Dr Clarke was born in England.Where was he born?
He immigrated to Sri Lanka in 1956.When did he immigrate to Sri Lanka?
He loved Sri Lanka because of its beauty.Why did he love Sri Lanka?
He lived there for 50 years.For how long did he live there?
  • Who asks about a person, What a thing, Where place, When time, Why reason, How many/much number/amount, For how long duration.
  • If the answer is the subject, no auxiliary is added: Who built it?
  • Start with a capital, end with “?”
💡

The 2020 official questions were: Who is Dr Arthur C. Clarke? / Where was he born? / What was his childhood dream? / When did he immigrate to Sri Lanka? / Why did he love Sri Lanka? / For how long did he live in Sri Lanka?

🧪 Self-check: Make a question: “The match was postponed because of rain.”

Why was the match postponed?
