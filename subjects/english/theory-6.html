👂 Homophones, synonyms and antonyms

Homophone setRule of thumb
two / to / tootwo = 2; to = direction/verb; too = also/excess
wear / wherewear clothes; where = place
right / writeright = correct/side; write = pen
nose / knowsnose = face; knows = understands
four / for / forefour = 4; for = purpose
their / there / they’retheir = belongs; there = place; they’re = they are
hear / herehear = ears; here = place
buy / by / byebuy = purchase
week / weakweek = 7 days; weak = not strong
  • Synonyms (same meaning): big–huge, begin–start, awful–extremely bad, explore–search a place.
  • Antonyms: success–failure, brave–cowardly, ancient–modern.
  • Plurals from Latin/Greek: erratum–errata, medium–media, axis–axes, curriculum–curricula.
  • Collective nouns: a flock of birds, a team of players, a bunch of keys. Compound nouns: toothpaste, ice-cream, bus stop.
💡

2021 Test 9 (Covid text) key: two, wear, right, nose, four — all homophones. Context decides: “wear a mask”, “cover your nose”.

Vocabulary tests 2 and 3

📦 Word-bank gap-fill (Tests 2 and 3)

  • Test 2 (5 marks): dialogue/notice/invitation with a word bank. Read the whole text first; each word is used once.
  • Test 3 (5 marks, 1/2 mark per gap): a picture + a short description; 10–11 gaps, 1/2 each. Use the picture to decide: nouns (table, ladder), adjectives (neat, small), -ing verbs (standing, checking).
  • Work out the word class for each gap; then fit the picture meaning.
  • Cross out a word once you use it. Spelling must be correct (capitals ignored).
