📝 Test 16 forms

  • Essay: intro · for · against · your view · conclusion

  • Story / dialogue: use the opening given · past tense · sequence · ending

🔤 Tenses

  • Present perfect: have/has + V3
  • Past perfect: had + V3
  • Present perfect continuous: have/has been + V-ing
  • Future perfect: will have + V3
  • Passive: be + V3; is being built; has been built

🔀 Conditionals · reported speech

  • Type 1: If + present, will + V
  • Type 2: If + past, would + V
  • Type 3: If + had + V3, would have + V3. Unless = if not
  • Reported: back-shift (is→was, will→would). Questions keep statement order: asked what the name was
  • Truths do not change: said the sun rises in the east

🔗 Conjunctions · prepositions

  • although/though: contrast in one sentence · however: new sentence + comma
  • whenever = every time · while = at the same time · because/as = reason
  • at + time/point · in + month/morning · during + period · for + duration · wait for

🧩 Pronouns · word forms

  • subject (they) · object (them) · possessive adjective (their)
  • each other = two · one another = more than two
  • Noun after the/a/of · adjective before noun or after be · adverb modifies verb · verb after to/modal
  • Homophones: two/to/too · wear/where · right/write · nose/knows · four/fore
