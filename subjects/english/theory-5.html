Memory trick — “AT is a dot, IN is a box, ON is a line”. AT = a point (at 6, at the gate); IN = inside space/time (in a room, in May); ON = a surface/day (on the wall, on Friday).

👤 Pronouns and possessives

  • Subject before the verb: They played well.
  • Object after verb/preposition: I saw them.
  • Possessive adjective before noun: their team.
  • each other (2 people) / one another (more than 2): The twins helped each other.
💡

2020 Test 4 key: You, They, their, them, it, we — a mix of all kinds. Ask: is the gap before a verb (subject), after a verb (object) or before a noun (possessive)?

⚠️

its (belongs to it) ≠ it’s (it is / it has). ✗ The dog wagged it’s tail.

🔤 Articles — a, an, the

ArticleRuleExample
aconsonant SOUND, one of many (first mention)a book, a university, a European
anvowel SOUNDan apple, an hour, an honest man
thespecific / already mentioned / uniquethe sun, the Principal, the book on the table
no articlegeneral plurals, uncountables, namesDogs bark. Honesty is best. Sri Lanka
⚠️

It depends on the sound, not the letter: an hour (silent h), a university (sounds like “you”).

🧪 Self-check: a / an / the: “___ umbrella I bought is ___ old one.”

The umbrella I bought is an old one. (the = specific umbrella; an = vowel sound in ‘old’.)
