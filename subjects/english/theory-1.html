⏱️ The tense map — pick the right form

Tests 10 and 12 give you a verb in brackets. You must change it to the correct form. First find the time clue, then choose the tense.

TensePatternTime cluesExample
Present simpleV1 (+s/es for he/she/it)every day, always, usually, general truthHonesty makes a person trusted (2025 T10)
Past simpleV2yesterday, last year, in 2019, agoHe left early (2024 T10)
Present continuousam/is/are + V-ingnow, at the moment, todayShe is flying today
Past continuouswas/were + V-ingwhile, at that time, when (background action)They were travelling when it rained (2022 T4)
Present perfecthave/has + V3already, yet, ever, never, since, for, so farHe has worked here since 2010 (2019 T12)
Past perfecthad + V3before, by the time, after (the earlier of two past actions)He had stayed in a hotel before he built a house
Present perfect continuoushave/has been + V-ingfor, since, all day, latelyI have been waiting for an hour
Past perfect continuoushad been + V-ingfor, since + earlier pastShe had been waiting for two hours when he came
Future simplewill + V1tomorrow, next week, soonYou will get a reply (2021 T12)
Future perfectwill have + V3by next year, by 2030By June I will have finished
💡

Memory trick — “PEN-HAT”: Perfect = HAve/HAs/HAd + V3. Continuous = BE + V-ing. Perfect continuous = HAVE + BEEN + V-ing.

🧪 Self-check: Choose: “She (live) here since 2015.”

has lived — “since” + a starting point needs present perfect.

⚠️

Common mistakes: (1) “He have gone” → he has gone. (2) Using V2 after has/have: “has went” ✗ → “has gone” ✓. (3) Forgetting -s: “She go” ✗ → “goes” ✓.

6.1 · active perfect continuous

🔁 Continuous and perfect continuous

Continuous

  • Action in progress: I am reading.
  • Past background: I was cooking when he called.
  • Use -ing after sees/hears/watches: saw planes flying

Perfect continuous

  • have/has been + V-ing — started in past, still going: I have been waiting for an hour.
  • had been + V-ing — before another past time.
  • Use for (duration) and since (start point).
forsince
for two hours, for a week, for ten yearssince 8 o’clock, since Monday, since 2015
