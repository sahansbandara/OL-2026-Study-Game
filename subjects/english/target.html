🎯 1 · Test 6 · Notice (5 marks)

A notice appeared in 6 of 7 papers (2019–2025); an announcement in 2023. Events at school are the usual theme.

The Sports Committee is organising the Inter-House Athletics Meet. Write a notice (40–50 words) including: date and time and place; what students must wear; to whom to give names for track events (and by when).
📌

Model answer (ආදර්ශ) — written by us, not an official marking-scheme answer. Write your own version first, then compare.

NOTICE
Inter-House Athletics Meet
The Sports Committee will hold the Inter-House Athletics Meet on Friday, 12 March, from 8.30 a.m. at the school ground. All students must wear their house T-shirts. Those who wish to take part in track events should give their names to Mr. Fernando, the sports master, before 5 March.
Secretary, Sports Committee

Body: 49 words (cap 40–50). All points covered.

🎯 2 · Test 8 · Paragraph (5 marks)

Paragraph topics are daily-life themes (food, sport, free time, a person). Practise past tense and present tense.

Write a paragraph (50–60 words): (a) My favourite festival or (b) A place I like to visit.
📌

Model answer (ආදර්ශ) — written by us, not an official marking-scheme answer. Write your own version first, then compare.

My favourite festival
My favourite festival is the Sinhala and Tamil New Year. Our whole family wakes up early and prepares sweets such as kavum and kokis. We light the hearth at the auspicious time and visit our relatives. Children play traditional games in the evening. I love this festival because it brings the family and the neighbours together.

56 words · topic sentence + 3 details + closing sentence.

🎯 3 · Test 14a · Formal letter (10 marks)

Formal letters appeared in 3 of 7 papers (2022, 2024, 2025), always from a club/society secretary or a customer. Expect one again.

You are the Secretary of the Language Club, Weldeniya Maha Vidyalaya. Write a letter (about 100 words) to the manager of a company asking for a donation of books. Include: why books are needed; what books; why you chose this company; how you will collect them.

Weldeniya.

The Manager,
Sunrise Books (Pvt) Ltd,

Dear Sir,
Request for a donation of library books

🎯 4 · Test 14b · Chart description (10 marks)

Bar charts 4 times and pie charts 3 times in 7 papers. Practise both. This chart is our own practice data, not from a past paper.

Describe the pie chart (about 100 words). Use: most, less than, more than, least.
After the exam: plans of 100 Grade 11 students
🟧 Vocational course 40
🟦 A/L 30
🟩 Job 20
🟥 Travel 10
📌

Model answer (ආදර්ශ) — written by us, not an official marking-scheme answer. Write your own version first, then compare.

The pie chart shows how 100 Grade 11 students plan to spend their time after the exam. Most students, 40 percent, want to start a vocational course. Thirty students plan to study for the Advanced Level, which is less than the first group. Twenty percent want to find a job. Only 10 students want to travel, so this is the least popular plan. The number of students who want to continue studies, either at A/L or in a vocational course, is more than double the number who want to work. In short, most students prefer to learn more before they begin to work.

103 words · introduces the chart, highest, comparisons, lowest, summary, uses figures.

🎯 5 · Test 16c · Speech (15 marks)

A speech option appeared in 7 of 7 papers (topics: good health habits, home gardening, Sri Lanka, reading, food). Environment is a safe theme.

Prepare a speech for the school assembly on The importance of protecting the environment (about 200 words).
📌

Model answer (ආදර්ශ) — written by us, not an official marking-scheme answer. Write your own version first, then compare.

The importance of protecting the environment

Respected principal, teachers and dear friends,
Good morning to all. I am very happy to speak about the importance of protecting our environment.
Our environment gives us clean air, water, food and shelter. Without trees, rivers and animals, life would be impossible. Yet today we cut forests, pollute rivers and throw plastic everywhere. As a result, we face floods, droughts and diseases.
What can we do? First, let us plant a tree every year and look after it. Second, let us stop using polythene and plastic bottles, and bring our own bags and bottles. Third, let us save water and electricity at home and at school.
Some people think one student cannot make a difference. That is not true. When every student acts, our school becomes greener, and then our village and our country. Even small actions, like picking up litter or switching off a fan, add up to a big change over the years.
So, friends, let us promise today to protect the earth for the children of tomorrow. Remember, we do not inherit the earth; we borrow it from them.
Thank you.

184 words · greeting, purpose, 3 points, persuasion, thank-you ending.

🎯 6 · Test 16d · Story (15 marks)

A story or dialogue option appears every year (2022: broke a neighbour's window; 2025: a dog saves a family). Learn to open, build up and close.

Write a story (about 200 words) about a time you broke something and owned up.
📌

Model answer (ආදර්ශ) — written by us, not an official marking-scheme answer. Write your own version first, then compare.

Owning up

It was the last day of the cricket season and the whole street was playing in the lane behind my house. I was batting and our team needed four runs to win. The bowler ran in and I swung hard. The ball flew high and crashed into Mr. Gunasekara's window with a loud noise.
Everyone froze. Then my friends ran away. I stood alone with the bat in my hand and my heart beating fast. I wanted to run too, but I knew it was wrong, and I remembered my mother's words about honesty.
I walked to the gate and rang the bell. An old man opened the door with an angry face. “I am very sorry, sir,” I said softly. “It was my mistake. I will pay for the window with my pocket money.”
He looked at me for a long time. Then he smiled and said, “Thank you for telling the truth. Come in, and let us clean the glass together.” Later my father repaired the window, and I helped him all week. I never forgot that day: owning up to a mistake is hard, but it makes you feel free and gives you the respect of others.

200 words · past tense · beginning, problem, ending, dialogue with inverted commas.

🎯 7 · Tests 10 and 12 · Mixed grammar

Verb forms, passive, conditionals, reported speech and conjunctions appeared in 7 of 7 papers.

A. Verb forms (Test 10 style). Write the correct form of the verbs in brackets.
My uncle (live) in Kandy. He (work) there since 2015. Last year he (visit) us and (bring) gifts. If I (get) good results, he (buy) me a bicycle.

B. Reported speech. (1) “I am tired,” said Kamal. (2) “Where do you live?” she asked me.

C. Conditional (type 3). If I (study) harder, I (pass) the test.

D. Passive. The workers are building the bridge. → The bridge …

E. Conjunction. ____ it rained heavily, we played cricket. (although / because)

📌

Model answer (ආදර්ශ) — written by us, not an official marking-scheme answer. Write your own version first, then compare.

B. (1) Kamal said that he was tired. (2) She asked me where I lived.
C. had studied … would have passed.

🎯 8 · Test 15 · Comprehension (8 marks)

Comprehension appeared 7 of 7 times: T/F, reference, synonym, short answer, title. This passage is our own practice text.

The Banyan Tree. Near our school stands an old banyan tree. Many years ago, villagers planted it beside the well. In summer, its wide branches give cool shade to travellers. Birds build nests among the leaves, and children play under it after school. Last year, a storm broke one large branch, but the tree did not die. Now the villagers care for it with great love.

1. True or False: (a) Travellers planted the tree. (b) Birds live in the tree. (c) The storm killed the tree. (d) Children play under it.
2. In “villagers planted it”, what does “it” refer to?
3. Find a word in the text that means “broad”.
4. What happened to the tree last year?
5. Give a suitable title.

📌

Model answer (ආදර්ශ) — written by us, not an official marking-scheme answer. Write your own version first, then compare.

1. (a) False (b) True (c) False (d) True
2. the (banyan) tree
3. wide
4. A storm broke one large branch (but the tree did not die).
5. e.g. “A Tree That Survived” / “The Old Banyan Tree”.
