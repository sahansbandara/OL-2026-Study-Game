📋 Notice and announcement (Test 6)

40–50 words. Marking: Content 2 + Language 3 = 5. A notice appeared 6 times, an announcement once (2023, railway).

NOTICE
Environment Day Plant Distribution
The Environmental Society will distribute plants on Environment Day, 5 June. Venue: school hall. Time: 9 a.m. All Grade 11 students may collect fruit and shade plants. Please bring a polythene bag.
Secretary, Environmental Society · 1 June

  • Heading (NOTICE) + title.
  • Who/what, when (date and time), where, instructions/what to bring.
  • Name + post of writer, date.
  • Include every guideline point (3 or 4 points); content 2 means all points.
  • 40–50 words; below 20 words = maximum 2 marks overall.

🌍

Announcement style (2023): “Attention please! The Udarata Menike will be delayed by one hour. It will now leave from platform 3 at 10.30 a.m. We apologise for the inconvenience.”

⚠️

Mistake: copying the question words as your notice earns 0 content. Rewrite in your own words.

🧪 Self-check: Which details are needed in a notice about a class trip?

Date and time, destination, meeting place, cost/what to bring, contact person, writer’s name and post.

🎯 How the question comes: “You are the Secretary of … Write a notice (40–50 words) including (a) date and time (b) place (c) what to bring.” Past topics: dengue clean-up (2020), lost pencil case (2021), children’s home visit (2022), plant distribution (2024), return of books (2025).
Test 8 · 5 marks Frequent in exams

📝 Paragraph (Test 8)

Choose 1 of 2 topics; 50–60 words. Content 2 + Language 3 (content 2 = ≥ 3 points).

Topic: The food I like most (2025)
The food I like most is rice and curry. My mother cooks it every Sunday. It has rice, dhal and a fish curry. It tastes delicious and keeps me healthy. I enjoy eating it with my family. That is why I like it most. (our sample)

  • Sentence 1 = topic sentence. 3+ different points. Last sentence = closing.
  • Use the right tense: the sportsmeet we had → past; the place where I live → present.
  • Aim for ~55 words; count them.
💡

Past topics: place where I live / free time (2019); a helpful person / a place I like to visit (2020); good health habits in class / unforgettable incident (2021); elderly people / TV programme (2022); using a library / best gift (2023); travelling / favourite sport (2024); favourite food / sportsmeet (2025).

🧪 Self-check: What is the ideal structure of a 55-word paragraph?

Topic sentence + 3 supporting points + closing sentence — about 5–6 sentences.

Test 16(d) · dialogue

💬 Completing a dialogue

2020 (Rizvi/Shenal), 2021 (Nimali/Kumari at the Book Fair) used a dialogue-completion option in Test 16.

  • Use speaker names with a colon, or new lines with inverted commas.
  • Informal, polite language: Hi! How are you? Guess what?
  • Match each answer to the previous line; keep a clear start and end.
  • Include all content given in the outline (place, activity, feelings, plan).
  • Check punctuation: ? ! , ‘ ’

Rizvi: Hi Shenal! What did you do in the holidays?
Shenal: I visited my grandmother in Kandy. How about you?
Rizvi: I went to Galle. We swam in the sea.
Shenal: That sounds great! (our sample)

🧪 Self-check: How do you start and end a dialogue?

Start with a greeting and topic; end with a closing remark (thanks, see you soon, plans).
