🔎 Reading Strategies — Tests 1, 5, 7, 11, 13

Reading carries 35 marks. Here are the techniques for the objective tests.

🖼️ Tests 1 & 13 matching📊 Test 5 transfer📖 Test 7 MCQ🧩 Test 11 cloze
Competency 5.1, 5.9 · Tests 1, 13 Frequent in exams

🖼️ Matching: signs, instructions, headings, adverts

Test 1 (5 marks): match pictures/signs/places with instructions. Item 1 is an example (6 items shown, 5 to match). Test 13 (5 marks): match 5 short extracts with headings, captions, ads or descriptions (6 options, one extra).

  • Underline keywords in each extract (dates, prices, verbs like mix, cook, discount, visit).
  • Identify the text type: recipe = steps and ingredients; advert = discount/visit/call; notice = dates and suspensions; guide = tips and advice.
  • Match the easiest first; cross off used options. One option is extra.
  • Write the letter only, clearly.

Next time you see a sign (WET FLOOR, NO ENTRY, HANDLE WITH CARE), say the instruction in your own words — that is Test 1.

🧪 Self-check: “Add the flour, stir well, bake for 20 minutes.” — heading or text type?

A recipe (instructions with verbs and times).

Competency 5.3 · Test 5 Frequent in exams

📊 Information transfer (Test 5)

Read a dialogue or factual text; complete a 6-row table (item 1 is usually an example). Each answer is a short phrase copied from the text.

  • Read the table headings first — they tell you what to look for.
  • Scan the text for the keyword (e.g. life span → “30–50 years”).
  • Copy words exactly; keep it short (1–4 words).
  • Numbers/dates must match the text.

Competency 5.2, 5.6, 5.8 · Test 7

📖 Story MCQs (Test 7)

  • 5 MCQs on a short narrative (about 1 mark each).
  • Read the passage twice: first for the story, then for details.
  • Look at the question stem and find the line evidence; do not use your own knowledge.
  • Eliminate options that are too extreme or only partly true.
  • Mood questions: decide the feeling from actions (smiling = cheerful; ‘How did I miss this place?’ = excited).

🧪 Self-check: “People walked slowly, smiling as they passed.” People are probably...

cheerful — actions show feelings (2025 key).

Test 11 · 7 marks Frequent in exams

🧩 Banked cloze (Test 11) — 7 marks

A passage with 14 gaps (1/2 mark each) and 15 words in a bank — one word is extra. Biggest single reading item after the comprehension.

  • Read the whole passage ignoring the gaps for the main idea.
  • For each gap, decide the grammar: preposition, article, verb form, linking word, noun, adjective.
  • Look for words before/after: to + verb, the + noun, and/but links.
  • Do easy gaps first, cross out words, finish with the leftover.
  • Check that the passage reads smoothly.
