✉️ Formal and informal letters (~100 words)

Marking: Content 3 + Language 3 + Organization/format 2 + Mechanics 2 = 10. Informal letters 4 times, formal 3 times in 2019–2025. Below 40 words = max 4 marks.

  • Include every guideline point in the same order, each with a few facts.
  • Informal = friendly, contractions fine; Formal = polite, no slang.
  • Sender address top right/left + date; receiver address for formal letters.
  • Closings: Yours sincerely (name known), Yours faithfully (Dear Sir/Madam).

Test 14(b) · 10 marks Frequent in exams

📊 Describing a bar or pie chart

  • Introduce: “The bar chart shows the activities of 100 Grade 11 students.”
  • Highest/lowest with figures: “Sports is the most popular with 40 students.”
  • Compare using the prompt words: more than, less than, equal, higher, lower, most, least.
  • Close with a summary sentence.
  • Use all the words given in the question (they are hints); ~100 words.

Test 16 · 15 marks Frequent in exams

📰 Test 16 — article, essay, speech, story (~200 words)

Choose ONE. Marking: Content 5 + Language 5 + Organization 2 + Mechanics 3 = 15. Below 50 words = max 5. Content 0 = no marks for the rest.

FormStructureLanguage
Articlecatchy title → intro → body paragraphs (problems / solutions / role of public) → conclusionschool-magazine/newspaper register
Essayintro → advantages → disadvantages → own view → conclusionformal, balanced
SpeechRespected principal, teachers and friends → purpose → points → thankspersuasive: we must, let us, imagine…
Storyopening (given) → events in order → problem → endingpast tense, sequence words (first, then, suddenly, finally)
Dialoguespeaker names + lines; start + endinformal, natural
  • Plan 2 minutes: write 3–4 headings from the guidelines first.
  • Cover all points in the question — 5 content marks.
  • Use linking words: however, in addition, therefore, as a result.
  • Spend 3–5 minutes proofreading: capitals, full stops, commas, spelling.

🧪 Self-check: Where do the 3 Mechanics marks come from?

Spelling, punctuation and clear handwriting (3 = no spelling errors, correct punctuation, clear writing).

🎯 How the question comes: “Write ONE of the following in about 200 words.” Always choose the form where the guidelines make planning easy (article or essay for most students).
Competency 2 · Mechanics

✅ Mechanics — the free marks

  • Capital letter: sentence start, names, days, months, I.
  • Full stop . Question mark ? Exclamation !
  • Comma in lists and after introductory words: However, …
  • Apostrophe: don’t, Nimal’s book (not book’s plural).
  • Inverted commas “ ” for speech (dialogue).
  • Spelling: use words you know — copy words from the question carefully.
  • Handwriting: clear, readable, margin.
