🔄 Active → Passive: the swap

In the active voice the doer comes first. In the passive voice the receiver of the action comes first, and the doer is often dropped.

Active: The workers built the bridge.
Passive: The bridge was built (by the workers).

Formula: object of active → subject of passive + be (in the same tense) + V3

TensePassive formExample
Present simpleam/is/are + V3Products are produced in factories (2020 T12)
Past simplewas/were + V3The books were distributed (2021 T10)
Present continuousam/is/are being + V3A new road is being built (2025 T12)
Past continuouswas/were being + V3The room was being painted
Present perfecthas/have been + V3The app has been designed (2021 T10)
Past perfecthad been + V3The work had been finished
Futurewill be + V3Teachers will be recruited (2021 T10)
Modalcan/must/should be + V3Instructions can be followed (2021 T10)
💡

Memory trick — “BE + V3”. Whatever the tense, the passive always ends in V3. Put the be-word in the tense of the original sentence.

🧪 Self-check: Change to passive: “They are repairing the school.”

The school is being repaired. (present continuous → is being + V3)

Rules Frequent in exams

🚫 Rules and traps

  • Intransitive verbs (no object) cannot be passive: arrive, come, go, happen, complain, die. 2025 T12 key: complained (active) — not “was complained”.
  • Use by + doer only when the doer is important: painted by Picasso.
  • Use passive when the doer is unknown/obvious: My bike was stolen.
  • Passive of a question: Was the letter posted?
⚠️

Trap: In Test 12 you may be given a verb that needs active form even though the passage is about a passive idea. Check whether the subject is the doer (active) or the receiver (passive) before writing.

Subject does the action → ACTIVESubject receives the action → PASSIVE
The manager complained about the noise.The noise was reported to the manager.
Students are building a model.A model is being built by students.

🧪 Self-check: Active or passive? “Many old men (decide) to start school.” (our example)

decided (active). They decide — they are the doers. 2021 Test 15 passage style.
